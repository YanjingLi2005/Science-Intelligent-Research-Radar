Classify the claim by type and role.

Claim Types:
- empirical_result: A quantitative result with numbers, percentages, or scores
- method_mechanism: Describes how a method or component works
- innovation: States a novel contribution or design choice
- hypothesis_relation: States a hypothesized relationship between variables
- generalization: Claims broader applicability beyond tested conditions
- causal_explanation: Explains why something happens (cause and effect)
- limitation_boundary: Acknowledges scope limits or failure conditions

Claim Roles:
- core_conclusion: The paper's central thesis or main finding
- sub_claim: A supporting claim that contributes to a larger conclusion
- experimental_support: Evidence from experiments that backs a claim
- boundary_condition: The specific conditions under which a claim holds
- method_component: A claim about a specific method element
- comparative: A claim that compares against baselines or prior work
